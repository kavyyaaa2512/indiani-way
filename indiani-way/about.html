OUR STORY

Where culture meets
contemporary

The Indiani Way was born from a simple belief — that the women who carry our culture deserve clothes that feel as considered as they are.

Every piece begins in our Vadodara atelier, where third-generation karigars draw, trace and embroider by hand. No shortcuts. No compromise. Only fabric, thread, and the quiet patience of craft.

Hand-crafted designer wear for the woman who wears her heritage with a modern soul.

Founder
& Creative Director

THE FOUNDER

Rooted in India,
designed for today

Every piece we create is a celebration of individuality, thoughtfully designed for those who embrace their true self with confidence. Rooted in India and inspired by its rich heritage, our creations blend traditional essence with a modern, contemporary spirit.

We proudly stand for Made in India — not just as a label, but as a promise. A promise of craftsmanship, culture, and creativity coming together to create something truly meaningful. Each outfit is carefully crafted, keeping in mind the evolving style of today's woman while honoring the beauty of our roots.

— Chandni Ratanpara

Founder & Creative Director
